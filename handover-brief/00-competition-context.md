# Competition context — The Ken's Case Competition 2026

## Theme
"Every business in India built on owning your attention, your habit, or your
indecision will discover that an AI agent has none of the three. This is the
Great Rewiring." (verbatim from the competition page)

## Timeline
- **10 September 2026** — Solution Assembly round: 10-question written
  submission only, no build, no demo. **Done — submitted.**
- **15 September 2026** — Shortlist announced; rails round opens (sandboxes,
  docs, office hours with the three rail partners). **We are shortlisted —
  this is where the next round begins.**
- **10 October 2026** — Live finale presentation to investors and rail
  partners. Exact format (time limit, hardware, live vs. recorded) was
  unannounced as of the last check (2026-09-09) — confirm current status
  before planning around it.

## Track entered
**Product Strategy** (locked at submission, cannot change). Product Strategy
teams finish with a fully specified build — complete flows, the segment map,
the wall they hit and why. (Product Build teams instead finish with a working
prototype on a partner sandbox — not applicable to us.) Both tracks are judged
on: quality of evidence, depth of the load-bearing rail, specificity of the
ask.

## Judging criteria (unweighted, as published)
Evidence, Creativity, Clarity, Feasibility, Thoroughness. No official weights
were published by the organiser.

## Judges
Unknown for 2026 as of last check — the organiser's page did not name judges.
(2025's competition, a different format, had a senior-operator/investor panel:
Sahil Barua/Delhivery, Soumya Rajan/Waterfield Advisors, Nipun Kalra/BCG X
India, Prof. Varun Nagaraj/SPJIMR — not confirmed as 2026's panel, don't rely
on it.) Check whether 2026 judges have been announced since 15 September.

## Rail partners (sponsor tech)
- **Pine Labs** (payments/authorisation) — Pine Labs Payments Protocol (P3P)
  for agent-to-agent autonomous UPI/card payments; **Grantex** for spend
  authorization/mandates and audit trail. This is our submission's declared
  load-bearing rail (see `01-submitted-answers.md` Q4/Q5).
- **Delhivery** (logistics) — opened test access to its Maps and MCP systems.
  Our submission explicitly disclaims any Delhivery fit ("no role, honestly")
  — there is no physical/logistics leg in this opening.
- **Gnani** (voice) — India-focused voice AI. Our submission names this as
  real-but-secondary: phone-based recovery against finance desks/TPAs in
  Indian languages, lowering cost per recovery, not carrying the mechanism.

Side-prize detail known: "prizes for the most innovative solutions across each
of the rails" — no further named categories found as of last check.

## Hard constraints/vetoes
None firm, except a steer (from earlier ideation, not an organiser rule) away
from a generic chatbot interface. Not itself a submission constraint — no
official vetoes were published by the organiser.
