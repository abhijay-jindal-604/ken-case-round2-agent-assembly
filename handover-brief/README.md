## One-paragraph summary

**"Paid Tuesday"** is a claims-recovery-and-advance desk: it pays a claimant
70–95% of a stuck or unfiled reimbursement (starting with corporate T&E
backlogs, extending to health insurance claims) within a day, underwritten off
the claim document plus the counterparty's own published policy — no insurer
or employer agreement required — and recovers from the payout when it lands.
The reconciled ledger of advances and recoveries becomes an audited,
permissionless record of how fast every employer/insurer actually pays,
positioned as a public settlement-speed board. Track entered: **Product
Strategy** (a fully specified build, not a working prototype).

## Why this idea, not the alternatives

Two other ideas ("Scope of Care" — an AI call-screening agent for elderly
parents, and "The Showing-Up Record" — a portable adherence record) were
generated and adversarially evaluated alongside this one before submission.
"Paid Tuesday" was ranked #1 by a fresh-context critique on Thoroughness and
Creativity (the two rubric criteria weighted highest). That decision is closed
and already acted on — the submission is in. This handover brief deliberately
does **not** carry forward the discarded alternatives or the generation
history behind this one, so the next round can be planned from the
submission's actual content and commitments, not from prior reasoning about
ideas that were never sent in.
